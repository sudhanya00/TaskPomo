Delete your account

The quickest way is in the app: Me, then Delete account at the bottom. If you no longer have the app, you can do it here.

Deleting removes, for good:

  • Your tasks, sessions, notes and streaks
  • Your points, freezes and points history, including scroll minutes
  • Your recaps, monthly notes and Wrapped
  • Your sign-in with TaskPomo

Nothing is kept afterwards. To keep a copy, export a CSV from the app first (Me, Export data as CSV). Deleting here doesn’t remove the app from your phone; uninstall it there.
